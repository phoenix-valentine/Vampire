# Changelog

## v0.1.0 (2026-11-27): the core, in plain shapes

First playable. One night of three minutes, one thumb, three variations of the core action:
Slaughter (walk into the blood), Feed (stand still to drink), Hunt (let no one escape).

- Blood is health and growth; claws swing by themselves; a full level bar offers one of three powers (nine powers, three levels each).
- Three human types: peasant, watchman, archer. Every strike has a wind-up or an aim line; no hit takes more than 30% of full blood.
- Synthesised sound, one per kind of event; a heartbeat when blood is low.
- Title, level-up, pause and end screens; the end screen names what killed you and can copy a report.
- Tools: single-file build, seeded simulation that replays exactly, three bots, a balance lab, and 61 automatic checks including the real page at three phone sizes.

Not checked by anyone yet: a real iPhone, Safari, how the sound actually sounds, and whether any of it is fun.
