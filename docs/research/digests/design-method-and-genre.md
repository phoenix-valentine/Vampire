# Design method and genre

[T] Twisting the Classics, [S0] Step 0, [TR] Trend Remix, [SR] Success Recipes, [GG] genre guide; VS, BR, HC, 20M teardowns (search summaries; nothing played). (M) unverified memory; (inference) mine.

## 1. Twists [T]

- Kinds (trap): A session shape: endless, timer, one-shot (no ramp); B run with choices (number-only upgrades; 10 to 15 suffice); C one smart verb (no decision); D automate one part (automating the fun); E rivals: ghost, bots, daily seed (online multiplayer); F combine two games (neither leads); G flip the role (flipped side lacks decisions); H theme (long story); I platform or input (controls fighting touch).
- Carry one. Keep the core action recognisable in seconds; cut controls.
- Keep it only if: one sentence; adds a decision, not a rule; one tier up at most.
- Test: plain core, then the smallest twist (3 upgrades, not 30; a flip: one level). 10 rounds: a new decision each round? One silent tester, told only the controls. 2 to 3 tweaks, then swap.
- Works: tester names the twist, runs differ, unasked replay. Fails: needs explaining; more fun switched off.
- (inference) Base is D+B; the vampire flip is G+H. Each variation: a different twist kind (as [S0] asks of variants), with an off-switch.

## 2. Define before building

- Core sentence: "[Action] to [goal], but [twist]"; one verb, one object [S0].
- One page: one main fun, three pillars, goal, one failure, one-tap restart, smallest first version, "not yet" list, biggest risk as the prototype's one question. Budget is scope, not money: plain shapes, one weekend [S0].
- Score 10 checks x 10: verb in 5 s; fun in 10 s; feedback per input; base plus one twist; session fits device; cheap failure; choice every minute; reason to return; readable look; spreadable. 80+ build; 65 to 79 fix the two lowest; below, change the idea [S0][SR]. Twist, choices, return separate lasting from faded.
- Kill: under 65 after one fix round; 2+ fails of [TR]'s 11-line filter; not fun for 60 s as plain shapes [S0].
- Genre: "lasting", on one hit's evidence [TR]; copies faded, 17 Steam hits in 2023, off the top-10 lists since [SR]; "very saturated", winners change one thing or out-polish [GG]. Needs: one decision-adding twist, handmade look, five-second-clip read.
- Recipe 4, run builder [SR]: simple action; pick 1 of 3 combining upgrades; run ends, unlocks stay; costly in systems and balance. Browser: 10 to 15 minute runs, one thumb. Phone hits: 83% under 5 minutes, 70% tap or drag only, effects-led sound; never joystick plus buttons.

## 5. Take and avoid

| Take | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Reward at the kill site: greed against danger (VS, BR) | Plateau where standing still wins (VS) |
| Attack costs mobility: feeding slows or roots (20M) | Effects over player or threats (VS) |
| Authored clock, named beats, hard end: dawn (VS, HC, 20M) | Stat-only cards, text walls (BR) |
| Short timed hunts, 5 s breather (BR) | Menus before the first move (BR, HC) |
| One resource: health, growth, price (BR) | Second thumb or facing-aim (20M, VS) |
| Pairing and next tier on card; free reroll (HC, 20M) | Permanent power; core tools sold (20M, HC) |
| One staged jackpot; visibly growing signature attack (VS, HC) | Darkness hiding threats (20M) |
| Hard enemy cap; harmless first enemy (VS, BR, HC) | Second system crowding the core (HC) |

## 6. Gaps

None of these: explains its rules by theme (scores 2 to 3); was built upright, one-thumb first; stays readable at its busiest; fits 1 to 5 minutes; starts without menus. Only BR keeps tension once the build works. (inference) None has prey that flees, or threat rising from the player's own actions.
